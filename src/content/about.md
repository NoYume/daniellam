I'm a computer science student at UT Austin concentrating in AI and robotics. In the [Robotic Interactive Intelligence Lab](https://robin-lab.cs.utexas.edu/) with Prof. Roberto Martín-Martín, I study how robots can manipulate objects without breaking them, and I led the RoboCasa implementation of OopsieVerse (RSS 2026).

On the engineering side I build AI agents that ship: voice and multi-agent planning on Salesforce Agentforce in summer 2026, and LLM developer tools for simulation engineers at Lockheed Martin in 2025.

I also lead simulation for Texas Marine Robotics' RoboBoat 2027 boat and mentor new researchers through Texas Robotics. Off the clock I travel with a camera; those photos live on the Shots page.
